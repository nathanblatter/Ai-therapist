1. Email_Student group_09.01.2026

Subject line: Research study: use an AI mental health support app for 8 weeks (compensation provided)

Hello,

The Information Systems research team that ran last year's AI support agent study is recruiting BYU students (18+) for the next phase: an 8-week study of what it is like to use a voice-enabled AI mental health support app in daily life.

What participation involves: a short online baseline survey; a 30-45 minute onboarding session; using the app from your own device as much or as little as you like for 8 weeks (we suggest about two short conversations per week); a 5-minute weekly survey; and an exit survey (some participants will also be invited to a short optional interview). Total structured time is about 2-3 hours over the study, plus whatever app use you choose.

Compensation is provided (SONA credit or payment, prorated by the parts you complete). This study is not therapy and the app is not a substitute for professional care. Participation is voluntary and confidential; conversations are automatically de-identified before researchers can view them.
