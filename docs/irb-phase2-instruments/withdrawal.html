Phase 2 Study Withdrawal / Pause Survey (Qualtrics) — DRAFT for IRB review

Administered online, on demand (approximately 1 minute). Linked from the participant's account page ("Leaving the study or need a break?"). Participation is voluntary and all questions may be skipped; submitting the survey updates the participant's study status (paused or withdrawn) and notifies the study coordinator. Participants enter their study ID (auto-filled when arriving from the personalized link).

  1. What is the main reason you are stepping away? (It takes too much time / It was not helpful to me / It made me feel worse or uncomfortable / I have privacy concerns / I had technical problems / My life circumstances changed / Other)
  2. Is there anything you would like the research team to know? (free text, optional). Responses describing distress are reviewed by the study team within one business day and follow the same adverse-experience review procedure as the weekly check-in.
  3. Would you like to fully withdraw from the study, or take a break with the option to return? (Withdraw fully / Pause - I may return later)
  4. What should happen to the information collected about you so far? (Keep my information for the research / Please also delete my information where possible). Per the consent form, data already included in analyses cannot always be removed; deletion requests are honored wherever possible and logged.

Rationale: structured withdrawal-reason capture addresses digital mental health's "law of attrition" — reasons for dropout are themselves study data, and a distress-driven withdrawal is surfaced to the team as a reportable adverse experience.
