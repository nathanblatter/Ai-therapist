Phase 2 Exit Survey and Exit Interview Guide — DRAFT for IRB review

Part 1: Exit Survey (Qualtrics, Week 8, ~20 minutes)

  1. PHQ-2 and GAD-2 items (final administration; see instruments)
  2. Overall, how valuable was having access to the AI support agent over the past 8 weeks? (1 Not at all valuable - 7 Extremely valuable)
  3. Compared with your first week, talking with the AI support agent now feels: (Much worse / Somewhat worse / About the same / Somewhat better / Much better)
  4. What did you use the AI support agent for most? (check all: everyday stress, relationships, school pressure, sleep, mood, loneliness, practicing conversations, curiosity, other)
  5. Working-alliance items (same 3 items as weekly check-in, plus:) "I would recommend the AI support agent to a friend going through a hard time." (1-7)
  6. Did you ever choose the AI agent over talking to a person? How do you feel about that? (free text)
  7. Did you ever feel too reliant on the AI support agent? (Not at all / A little / Somewhat / Very much; optional free text)
  8. Was there ever a moment the AI support agent said something unhelpful or upsetting? Please describe. (free text)
  9. Did you ever discuss a serious personal crisis with the agent? If so, how did it respond? (free text, optional)
  10. How private did your conversations feel? (1 Not at all private - 7 Completely private)
  11. If this tool were available after the study, would you keep using it? (Definitely not - Definitely yes, 5-point)
  12. What would you change about the AI support agent? (free text)

Part 2: Exit Interview Guide (subsample, ~30 minutes, Zoom or in-person)

Semi-structured; interviewer follows the guide, probing as appropriate. Recorded; transcribed with study codes only.

  1. Walk me through a typical conversation you had with the AI support agent. What did you talk about, and how did it go?
  2. How did your use change over the eight weeks? What drove that change?
  3. Tell me about a time the agent was genuinely helpful. What made it helpful?
  4. Tell me about a time it fell short or felt off. What happened?
  5. Did talking with the agent change how, or whether, you sought support from people?
  6. Voice versus text: which did you prefer, and why?
  7. How did you think about privacy while using it?
  8. If you could design the ideal version of this tool, what would it do? What should it never do?
  9. Is there anything the research team should know that we have not asked about?
