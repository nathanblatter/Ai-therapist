GAD-2 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-2)

Administered in-app by the AI support agent, no more than once per week. Public domain (Kroenke, Spitzer, Williams & Loewe). Screener only; not a diagnosis.

Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following?

  1. Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge
  2. Not being able to stop or control worrying

Response options (per item): Not at all (0) • Several days (1) • More than half the days (2) • Nearly every day (3)

Score = sum of items (0-6). Conventional screen-positive cutoff: 3 or greater.

Reference: Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JB, Monahan PO, Loewe B. Anxiety disorders in primary care: prevalence, impairment, comorbidity, and detection. Annals of Internal Medicine. 2007;146(5):317-325.
