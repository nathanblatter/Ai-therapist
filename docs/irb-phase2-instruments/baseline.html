Phase 2 Baseline Survey (Qualtrics) — DRAFT for IRB review

Administered online at Week 0, after eligibility screening and informed consent. Adapted from the Phase 1 (2025-519) student demographic survey. Participants may skip any question they prefer not to answer (except eligibility items).

Section A. Eligibility confirmation

  1. Are you 18 years of age or older? (Yes / No — under-18 respondents cannot continue)
  2. Are you currently located in the United States, and do you expect to remain in the US for the next 10 weeks? (Yes / No — non-US respondents cannot continue)
  3. Are you currently a BYU student? (Yes / No)

Section B. Demographics

  1. Age
  2. Gender (Woman / Man / Nonbinary / Prefer to self-describe / Prefer not to say)
  3. Year in school (Freshman / Sophomore / Junior / Senior / Graduate)
  4. Major or field of study (free text)
  5. Race/ethnicity (check all that apply; standard categories; Prefer not to say)
  6. Is English your first language? (Yes / No)

Section C. Mental health background (all items optional)

  1. Have you ever attended therapy or counseling? (Never / In the past / Currently)
  2. If yes: approximately how long, in total? (Less than 3 months / 3-12 months / 1-3 years / More than 3 years)
  3. How would you rate your overall mental well-being over the past month? (1 Very poor - 7 Excellent)
  4. PHQ-2 items (see PHQ-2 instrument)
  5. GAD-2 items (see GAD-2 instrument)
  6. Where do you currently turn for emotional support? (check all: friends, family, therapist/counselor, clergy, apps or online tools, journaling, no one, other)

Section D. Technology and AI attitudes

  1. How often do you use AI assistants (e.g., ChatGPT, voice assistants)? (Never / Monthly / Weekly / Daily)
  2. Have you ever used an AI tool to talk about feelings, stress, or personal problems? (Yes / No)
  3. How comfortable would you be talking with an AI support agent about everyday stress? (1 Very uncomfortable - 7 Very comfortable)
  4. How comfortable would you be talking with an AI support agent about more personal difficulties? (1-7)
  5. To what extent do you trust an AI support agent to keep your conversations private? (1 Not at all - 7 Completely)
  6. What do you hope an AI support agent could help you with, if anything? (free text)
  7. What concerns, if any, do you have about talking with an AI support agent? (free text)

Contact information (added 2026-09-04): "What email address should the research team use to schedule your onboarding session and send survey reminders? (Used only for study communication; never shared.)" — required, validated email. Used to (a) schedule the onboarding session promised at the end of the survey and (b) send weekly/exit/follow-up survey reminder emails through BYU's Qualtrics platform. The address is stored in the survey response and in an access-controlled Qualtrics contact list managed by the research team; it is never included in de-identified research datasets.
