[[Block: Consent]]

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:CONSENTTEXT]]
<b>Consent to Be a Research Subject</b><br><br><b>Title:</b> Human + AI Therapy: Better For Everyone (Phase 2: Longitudinal Use)<br><b>Principal Investigator:</b> James Gaskin, PhD, Department of Information Systems, Brigham Young University<br><br><b>Introduction</b><br>This research study is being conducted by Professor James Gaskin and a research team at Brigham Young University to understand what it is like to use an AI mental health support agent over an extended period. You were invited to participate because you are a BYU student, at least 18 years old, located in the United States.<br><br><b>What is the AI support agent, and what is it not?</b><br>The AI support agent is a research tool that offers supportive, evidence-informed self-help conversation by voice or text. It is not a therapist, doctor, or crisis service, and this study is not therapy. It cannot diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for professional mental health care.<br><br><b>Procedures</b><br>If you agree to participate: (1) complete this baseline survey (about 15 minutes); (2) attend a 30-45 minute onboarding session with a research assistant (SONA lab or Zoom); (3) use the AI support agent from your own device as much or as little as you wish for 8 weeks (we suggest about two short conversations per week); (4) complete a 5-minute weekly check-in survey; (5) complete an exit survey (about 20 minutes) at Week 8; and (6) complete one short follow-up survey at Week 12. Some participants will be invited to an optional 30-minute recorded exit interview. In the app, the agent will occasionally ask two brief wellbeing questionnaires (PHQ-2 and GAD-2), no more than once per week each.<br><br><b>Recording and data collection</b><br>Your conversations with the agent (text, and transcriptions of voice conversations) are collected for research. Voice sessions are recorded as audio and stored encrypted; recordings are used for safety review and transcription verification and are deleted once transcription is verified. Before any researcher can read your conversations, an automated system removes identifying information, and a trained research assistant double-checks the removal; researchers see only the de-identified version. Original unredacted content is automatically deleted after a short retention period. The app also records when and how long you use it.<br><br><b>Risks and discomforts</b><br>Talking or writing about your mood and personal difficulties may bring up uncomfortable feelings. Although unlikely, it is possible that the chatbot interactions may worsen your condition, or may trigger a crisis episode. There is a small risk of over-relying on a tool that is not a clinical service; the agent will remind you of its limits. As with any digital system, a breach of confidentiality is possible; extensive safeguards make this very unlikely.<br><br><b>Safety monitoring and mandatory reporting</b><br>The system automatically monitors conversations for signs of a mental health crisis. If it detects that you may be in danger, it will show you crisis resources immediately and alert a trained on-call researcher. If you share information regarding ongoing abuse, neglect, sexual exploitation, or risk of harm toward yourself or others, we are required to report this to legal authorities, along with information about your identity. This could result in the arrest and prosecution of the perpetrators.<br><br><b>Benefits</b><br>There are no guaranteed direct benefits to you. Society may benefit from evidence about whether AI support tools can safely complement human mental health care.<br><br><b>Confidentiality</b><br>Research data are stored encrypted in access-controlled systems available only to the approved research team. De-identified data will be kept for future research and may be shared for publication or meta-analysis; your identity will not be shared. Your NetID, if collected for SONA credit, is handled by the SONA lab separately from research data. Conversations are processed by our AI service provider under contractual privacy protections; no conversation data is used to train commercial AI models.<br><br><b>Compensation</b><br>Compensation is prorated by what you complete: $10 (or 1 SONA credit) for baseline and onboarding; $3 (or SONA equivalent) per weekly check-in; $15 for the exit survey; $10 for the optional exit interview; $5 for the Week 12 follow-up (maximum about $64). If you withdraw early, you keep everything already earned.<br><br><b>Participation</b><br>Participation is voluntary. You may skip questions, pause, or withdraw at any time without penalty and without any effect on your standing at BYU or in any course. To withdraw, stop using the app and notify the research team; you may also request removal of your stored conversation data.<br><br><b>Questions about the Research</b><br>Contact Principal Investigator James Gaskin at james.gaskin@byu.edu or Nathan Blatter at nzb22@byu.edu.<br><br><b>Questions about Your Rights as a Research Participant</b><br>Contact the BYU Human Research Protection Program at (801) 422-1461 or BYU.HRPP@byu.edu.
