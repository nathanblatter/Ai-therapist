# Therapist tutorial narration (2026-09-04). Slides from tutorial-screenshots/.

1. [title-therapist] Welcome to the research portal. This tour covers the therapist role — clinical oversight, with the full clinical data tier. Everything you'll see is a practice sandbox with synthetic clients; your own account starts in exactly such a sandbox.

2. [t01-caseload] Your landing page is your caseload: every client assigned to you, plus one-time invite links that register a new client directly to you. Everything else in the portal is scoped to these people.

3. [t02-triage] Triage ranks your roster by who needs attention: open crisis flags, escalations, safety plans, low check-in moods, and overdue practice all surface as chips, most urgent on top. Start here each day.

4. [t03-participant-profile] Clicking a client opens the profile — the clinical heart of the portal. Screener trends and mood up top, then an AI-generated summary — always verify it against the record — and the session timeline with crisis tags, safety-plan events, and follow-ups. This is also where you start a progress note from the AI's SOAP draft, and where signing makes a note immutable.

5. [t04-work-queue] The work queue is where everything needing a human lands: crisis flags, inbound escalations, worsening screeners, stale messages. Ack claims an item; Resolve closes it with a note for the audit trail. Set your notification preferences below — keep urgent safety emails on.

6. [t05-escalations] Escalations are structured hand-offs from caseworkers — like this one: passive ideation disclosed, clinical follow-up requested. Acknowledge, work it, resolve with a disposition. After crisis flags, open escalations are your first priority.

7. [t06-messages] Messages is secure, asynchronous contact with your clients, started from their profile. Participants expect replies within one to two business days, and every incoming message is safety-scanned automatically — the inbox is never the only net. Also in your nav: live monitoring, full session history, analytics, crisis management, and adverse-event review. Practice it all in the sandbox first — nothing there reaches real participants.
