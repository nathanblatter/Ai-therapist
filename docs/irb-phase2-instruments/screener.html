Phase 2 Eligibility Screening Survey (Qualtrics) — DRAFT

Completed online before consent. Ineligible respondents are routed to a warm-referral end screen and their responses are deleted when recruitment closes.

  1. Are you 18 years of age or older? (Yes / No) — No ends the survey.
  2. Are you currently located in the United States, and do you expect to remain in the US for the next 10 weeks? (Yes / No) — No ends the survey.
  3. Are you currently a BYU student? (Yes / No) — No ends the survey.
  4. Do you have your own smartphone or computer with internet access that you can use for this study? (Yes / No) — No ends the survey.
  5. Are you currently experiencing a mental health crisis? (Yes / No)
  6. In the past 6 months, have you been hospitalized for a mental health concern? (Yes / No)
  7. In the past month, have you had thoughts of killing yourself with a specific plan or intent to act on them? (Yes / No)

Routing: A "Yes" on items 5-7 routes to the referral end screen: "Thank you for your interest. This study is not a substitute for professional care, and based on your answers we are not able to enroll you right now. Please reach out to people who can help: BYU Counseling and Psychological Services (801-422-3035; after-hours crisis line via BYU Police 801-422-2222), the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988), or the Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741). If you are in immediate danger, call 911."

Eligible respondents continue directly to the informed consent form.
