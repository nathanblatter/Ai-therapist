Phase 2 Weekly Check-in Survey (Qualtrics) — DRAFT for IRB review

Administered online once per week during Weeks 1-8 (approximately 5 minutes). Participants may skip any question. Participants enter their study ID (auto-filled when arriving from a personalized link).

  1. About how many times did you talk with the AI support agent this week? (0 / 1 / 2-3 / 4-6 / 7 or more)
  2. Which did you mostly use this week? (Voice / Text / Both about equally / Did not use it this week)
  3. This week, how helpful were your conversations with the AI support agent? (Not at all helpful / Slightly / Moderately / Very / Extremely helpful; N/A did not use)
  4. Working-alliance items (6 investigator-developed statements adapted from the working-alliance construct: Task, Bond, and Goal dimensions, 2 items each; 5-point scale, Strongly disagree - Strongly agree; participants who did not use the agent select Neutral):
    1. The AI support agent understands what matters to me. (Bond)
    2. I feel comfortable being open with the AI support agent. (Bond)
    3. The AI support agent and I are working toward things I care about. (Goal)
    4. The AI support agent and I agree on what to work on in our conversations. (Task)
    5. The way the AI support agent and I work on my concerns feels right for me. (Task)
    6. The AI support agent and I agree on what I want to get out of using it. (Goal)
  5. How would you rate your overall mood this week? (Very poor / Poor / Fair / Good / Very good / Excellent)
  6. How stressed did you feel this week? (Not at all / Slightly / Moderately / Very / Extremely)
  7. Did anything about your conversations with the AI support agent bother you, upset you, or feel wrong this week? (No / Yes - please describe, free text). Responses describing distress are reviewed by the study team within one business day; participants in need are contacted with support resources. An inline notice with crisis resources is shown when "Yes" is selected.
  8. Did you seek support from a person (friend, family, counselor) for something you also discussed with the AI agent this week? (Yes / No / Prefer not to say)
  9. Anything else you want the research team to know this week? (free text, optional)
