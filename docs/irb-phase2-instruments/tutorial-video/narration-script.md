# App tutorial video — narration script (2026-09-04)

Voice: OpenAI TTS "sage" (calm), ~140 wpm. One segment per slide; slide = screenshot from tutorial-screenshots/.

1. [01-home] Welcome to the AI Support Agent study. This short video shows you around the app. One thing before we start: the AI support agent is not a therapist, doctor, or crisis service. If you ever need real support, the Call 988 and Crisis Resources buttons are at the top of every screen, any time.

2. [01-home] This is your home screen. The Start Session button begins a conversation with the AI. Next to it, the gear icon opens your settings. Along the top you'll find crisis resources, your private messages, and your profile. And in the corner, Report a Problem sends a note straight to the team if anything ever looks broken.

3. [10-home-progress] After your first few conversations, the home screen shows a simple progress card — how often you've talked in recent weeks. These are brief check-ins over time. They are not a diagnosis, and they are not a grade.

4. [04-session-consent] The first time you start a session, the app briefly restates what you agreed to in your consent form: conversations are transcribed for the study, sessions may be monitored, voice sessions are recorded, and if you ever seem to be in danger, the crisis protocol shows you resources and may notify the team. Tick the box and press I agree to continue.

5. [05-checkin] Before each conversation there's a quick optional check-in: how you're feeling, what's on your mind, and what you'd like to get out of today. It helps the conversation start where you are — but you can skip any of it, or all of it. There are no wrong answers.

6. [06-live-session] This is a live session. You can talk or type — your choice, any time. Your microphone starts off; press the mic button when you want to speak, or just use the message bar. Notice the timer counting down so the ending never surprises you, and the recording badge, which is visible whenever audio is being recorded. The Disconnect button ends the conversation whenever you want — you are always free to stop.

7. [07-feedback] When a session ends, three quick ratings and an optional comment. Fifteen seconds, and it genuinely shapes the research. You can also skip it.

8. [02-settings-voice-appearance] Now the settings. You can pick from ten voices — press the small speaker icon to preview each one. Every voice is the same agent with the same safeguards, so choose whatever is most pleasant to listen to. Below that, five color themes. These are comfort preferences only; they change nothing about the study.

9. [03-settings-accessibility-memory] Further down: accessibility. Text size, reduced motion, high contrast, and a dyslexia-friendly font. And then session memory — the one setting that changes what the AI remembers. When it's on, the AI keeps a short thematic summary of each conversation, never a transcript, and uses it for continuity next time. When it's off, every conversation starts fresh. Switch it any time.

10. [08-profile] Your profile shows your account, session statistics, and history. It's also where study options live — including a link to pause or leave the study. That's always allowed, never penalized, and you keep everything you've already earned.

11. [11-messages] Messages is a private, unhurried way to reach the research team. Replies come within a business day or two — it is not monitored in real time, so if you need help right now, use 988.

12. [09-mic-error] If you ever see this microphone message, your browser just needs permission — click the icon by the web address and allow the microphone. Or simply type instead; everything works by text. And that's the app. Use it as much or as little as feels right — we suggest a couple of short conversations a week. Thanks for being part of the study.
