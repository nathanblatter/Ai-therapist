PHQ-2 (Patient Health Questionnaire-2)

Administered in-app by the AI support agent, no more than once per week. Public domain (Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams). Screener only; not a diagnosis.

Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following?

  1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things
  2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless

Response options (per item): Not at all (0) • Several days (1) • More than half the days (2) • Nearly every day (3)

Score = sum of items (0-6). Conventional screen-positive cutoff: 3 or greater.

Reference: Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JB. The Patient Health Questionnaire-2: validity of a two-item depression screener. Medical Care. 2003;41(11):1284-1292.
